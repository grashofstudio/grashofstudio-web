# SEO and Quality Requirements

- Unique title and description for every page
- Canonical URL on every page
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- Organization JSON-LD
- `robots.txt`
- `/sitemap.xml`
- Branded social preview image
- Semantic headings and navigation
- No broken local links or missing assets
- Future Systems visibly labeled R&D and Under Development
- No invented client names, performance metrics, or commercialization claims

## Review cycle

After Google has had time to crawl the release, compare consistent 28-day periods in Search Console. Check index status for each topic, then impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by query/page/country. Suggested query filter: `digital twin|thermal|satellite|數位孿生|熱流|熱管理|衛星`. If query rows remain suppressed, use page totals and record the limitation; do not invent query rankings.

Prioritize concrete buyer queries (e.g. thermal digital twin development, thermal-fluid solutions Taiwan, satellite thermal management R&D) alongside the broad requested terms. The English and Chinese market priorities may be refined by actual demand; no search-volume estimates are established here.

Next evidence needed: publishable model-validation material, actual sensor/model correlation, approved author credentials, and later hardware test results. The 2026-10-05 content uses only high-level satellite progress from “Phase 0 task” and the Mac reproduction report: orbital-load/offline software groundwork exists; integrated thermal acceptance remains pending. It does not publish private model files, administrative blockers, or proposed hardware product dates.

External promotion (company LinkedIn, industry directories, partner references) needs truthful profiles and permission to publish; it is not performed by this website release. Paid search needs a separately approved budget and campaign scope. Buying backlinks or claiming guaranteed top-three rankings is out of scope.
